# Creator Analytics Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

## Overview

Creator Analytics Bot is a personal analytics application designed to help creators monitor social media performance and track growth over time.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may access and how that information is used.

## Information Collected

Creator Analytics Bot may collect analytics information from connected platforms, including:

- Account identifiers
- Username information
- Subscriber or follower counts
- View counts
- Like counts
- Video statistics
- Performance history
- Growth metrics

The application only requests information necessary to provide analytics features.

## How Information Is Used

Collected information is used to:

- Display creator analytics
- Track growth trends
- Generate reports
- Create performance charts
- Identify changes in content performance

## Data Storage

Analytics data may be stored locally by the application to maintain historical records and generate reports.

The application does not store passwords.

Authentication information, such as access tokens, is stored securely and is only used to communicate with connected platforms.

## Data Sharing

Creator Analytics Bot does not:

- Sell personal information
- Share user data with advertisers
- Share data with unrelated third parties

Data is only used to provide the application's analytics functionality.

## Third-Party Platforms

Creator Analytics Bot may connect with third-party services, including:

- YouTube
- TikTok
- Discord

These platforms have their own privacy policies and terms of service that govern their services.

## User Control

Users can:

- Disconnect their accounts
- Revoke application permissions
- Request removal of stored analytics data

## Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application changes.
